Privacy policy

WallCraft Pro runs entirely on your PC. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

Download v2026.11.13 for Windows Learn how it works

What WallCraft does not do

WallCraft Pro requires no account and sends nothing over the Internet: not your images, not usage statistics, not error reports.

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • No data shared with third parties.
  • No network connections made by the app itself.

What stays on your PC

To work, WallCraft saves a few files in your Windows app data folder. They never leave your PC and are removed with the app.

  • Your settings (language, real screen size, automatic wallpaper options).
  • The applied wallpaper images, one per screen, for each screen configuration.
  • Projects and exports, only where you choose to save them.

Images and face detection

The images you add are read and processed on your PC. In Fill mode, WallCraft finds where faces are to crop around them: this runs locally, does not identify anyone, and nothing is kept or sent.

Links and Microsoft Store

Links in the app (website, GitHub, Ko-fi) open in your browser; those sites apply their own privacy policies.

If you install WallCraft from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process installation data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. WallCraft has no access to it beyond the anonymous statistics Microsoft gives publishers.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. Like any host, the server may keep technical access logs for security.

Contact

For any question about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub page: github.com/rangornrar/MultiScreenWallpaper/issues.
