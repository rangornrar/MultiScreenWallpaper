The geometry challenge

When one screen is taller, wider, or rotated, the challenge is no longer just the photo.

A good setup acknowledges that the monitors do not form one perfect rectangle.

The best workflow adapts to uneven geometry instead of flattening everything into one crop.

  • Different resolutions.
  • Different physical heights.
  • Different aspect ratios.

WallCraft's approach

It treats the monitor map as part of the composition workflow.

This ensures your wallpaper feels intentional on your real desk.

Optimized layouts

Choose the algorithm that best matches your monitor arrangement.

Maximum optimization

Best when reducing visible bars is the highest priority.

Justified lines

Good when you want a more editorial rhythm.

Grid or strips

Useful when visual order matters more than aggressive packing.
